About

PhD candidate in Electrical Engineering at North Carolina State University (2022–2026), advised by Prof. Robert W. Heath Jr. My research combines optical and wireless communication theory — primarily radio-over-fiber and MIMO signal processing — to design higher-capacity fronthaul links for next-generation radio access networks (RAN). Previously an R&D engineering intern at Qorvo (RF/SAW filter design) and a research assistant modeling Li-ion battery state-of-health at LUMS.
